1. WHAT DATUM IS
================================================================================

1.1 THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
-------------------------
In multi-billion-dollar industrial megaprojects (oil and gas refineries, chemical
plants, power stations, and metro rail systems), project progress is formally
planned down to Level 5 and Level 6 (L5/L6) milestone deliverables in Oracle
Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project. However, daily field execution is captured in
unstructured, fragmented formats:
  - Daily Progress Reports (DPR) written in free-text by site supervisors
    (e.g., "CW 24-inch spool was erected near Pump Bay today; alignment in progress.")
  - Discipline-specific spreadsheets (e.g., piping welder logs with inch-dia joints).
  - Physical shift diaries and radio/verbal shift handovers.

Because site foremen use colloquial field jargon, informal acronyms, and partial
equipment tag numbers, project controls teams spend 15 to 25 hours every week
manually reading supervisor logs, figuring out which Primavera P6 activity they
belong to, and typing progress into scheduling software.

Consequences of the manual approach:
  1. Latency & Blind Spots: Planners are 3 to 7 days behind real jobsite reality.
  2. Inaccurate Delay Tracking: Delays are recognized too late for proactive mitigation.
  3. Ghost Work & Scope Creep: Out-of-scope rework goes untracked, leading to claims.
  4. Lack of Auditability: In disputes, no immutable evidence connects the supervisor's
     written log to the approved schedule update.

1.2 THE EVIDENCE-TO-SCHEDULE WORKFLOW
-------------------------------------
Datum solves this bottleneck through a deterministic, traceable pipeline:

  [Field Report + Photo Proof]
             │
             ▼
  [Browser-Local SHA-256 Fingerprint + OCR Tag Detection]
             │
             ▼
  [Human Verification / Confirmation of Equipment Tag]
             │
             ▼
  [Deterministic Multi-Factor Matching (Keyword + Discipline + Spatial + Fuzzy)]
             │
             ▼
  [Planner Review & Decision Workbench (Approve / Relink / Unplanned / Reject)]
             │
             ▼
  [Enriched Schedule Baseline (Actual Dates, % Complete, Variance Tracking)]
             │
             ▼
  [Forward Delay Propagation & What-If Scenario Risk Simulator]

1.3 WHO USES DATUM?
-------------------
  - Site Supervisors: Foremen and discipline leads capturing daily logs, photos,
    and equipment tags directly from the workfront.
  - Lead Planners / Project Controls: Schedulers verifying matching evidence,
    approving links, editing parameters, and managing scope variances.
  - Project Managers & Executives: Leadership teams monitoring critical paths,
    contractor claims, and overall portfolio milestones.
  - Auditors & Evaluators: Reviewers verifying that every schedule progression is
    backed by verifiable text, timestamp, and photo integrity proof.

2. CORE PROJECT CONTROLS TERMINOLOGY
================================================================================

  - L5 / L6 Activities:
    Level 5 / Level 6 Schedule Activities representing the most granular,
    day-to-day deliverables in a project schedule
    (e.g., "PIP-L6-012: Erect Line 24-CW-017 Spool & Flanges").

  - WBS (Work Breakdown Structure):
    A hierarchical decomposition of total project scope into manageable sections
    (e.g., "1.2.4.1: Cooling Water Piping").

  - Schedule Baseline:
    The approved, frozen project plan containing planned start dates, finish dates,
    and durations against which all actual execution is measured.

  - Variance (Days):
    The difference between actual execution dates and planned dates. A negative
    variance (e.g., -5 days) indicates execution finished ahead of plan; a positive
    variance (e.g., +7 days) indicates delay.

  - Confidence Score (%):
    A contextual metric (0-100%) indicating how strongly a specific field report
    matches a schedule activity based on keywords, tags, discipline, spatial area,
    and wording similarity. Confidence is evaluated per report, not as a static global number.

  - Unplanned Work:
    Field activities or scope performed on site that do not map to any approved
    baseline milestone, requiring planner investigation or change order authorization.

  - Integrity Fingerprint (SHA-256):
    A unique 64-character digital fingerprint computed directly in the browser
    from an attached photo file to guarantee provenance and audit integrity.

  - Audit Trail:
    An immutable chronological log recording every ingestion, OCR scan, tag
    confirmation, planner approval, parameter edit, and relinking decision.

3. ROLES AND PERMISSIONS
================================================================================

Datum enforces strict role-based operational boundaries. The active role is
toggled using the top-right header button ("Lead Planner Mode" / "Supervisor Mode").

3.1 SITE SUPERVISOR ROLE
------------------------
  CAN DO:
    - Ingest new daily progress records via the Supervisor Hub.
    - Attach construction photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP <= 8 MB) and run browser-local OCR scans.
    - Select or manually enter equipment tags (e.g., "24-CW-017", "18-FW-008", "PT-2401").
    - Flag site blockers, access holds, or safety hazards with severity levels (Low, Med, Critical).
    - View the Daily Field Reports board/table and inspect full record provenance in read-only mode.

  CANNOT DO:
    - Cannot approve, relink, or reject schedule matches.
    - Cannot alter baseline milestone dates, WBS items, or schedule dependencies.
    - Cannot access the Lead Planner Review Workbench, Data Ingestion schemas, or
      Executive Risk Simulator.

3.2 LEAD PLANNER ROLE
---------------------
  CAN DO:
    - Full authority to approve, relink to alternative milestones, classify as
      unplanned, or reject field reports.
    - Edit site report parameters (extracted description, area, event status,
      report date) in the Inspector Drawer.
    - Confirm or correct OCR equipment tags directly from the review workbench or inspector.
    - Filter the review queue by confidence level, discipline, or status.
    - Simulate delay propagation scenarios on critical path activities in the AI Copilot.
    - Batch ingest Primavera P6 schedule CSVs, supervisor DPR TXT logs, and contractor Excel spreadsheets.
    - Export the complete alignment matrix and audit log to standard CSV format.

4. SCREEN-BY-SCREEN USER GUIDE
================================================================================

4.1 PROJECT CONTROL CENTER (Dashboard)
--------------------------------------
  - Purpose: Executive dashboard providing real-time KPI metrics across field ingestion,
    matching categories, schedule variances, and discipline breakdowns.
  - What You See:
      * Top summary metric cards: Total Site Updates, Approved Baseline Links,

      * Discipline Breakdown: Item counts across Piping, Civil, Electrical, Instrumentation, HSE.
      * Recent Audit Activity: Live stream of latest planner decisions and supervisor entries.
  - How to Use: Click any metric card or category pill to automatically navigate
    with pre-filtered context.

4.2 DAILY FIELD REPORTS FEED (Site Updates)
-------------------------------------------
  - Purpose: Operational command view for browsing, filtering, searching, and
    inspecting all field reports ingested into Datum.
  - What You See:
      * Toggle between Kanban Board (Ready to Link, Needs Review, Approved Link,
        Unplanned Scope) and Table List View.
      * Search bar supporting multi-attribute search across Update ID, text,
        discipline, area, supervisor, blocker flags, and matched activities.
      * Filter chips for Discipline (Piping, Civil, Electrical, Instrumentation, HSE),
        Status, and Source File.
      * Compact badges indicating photo proof attachments with confirmed equipment
        tag tooltips ("[Photo: 24-CW-017]").
  - How to Use: Click any card or row to open the Inspector Drawer. Planners can
    click "Review ->" to jump directly to the Planner Review Workbench.

4.3 MILESTONE BASELINE (L5/L6 Schedule Activities)
--------------------------------------------------
  - Purpose: Interactive master schedule viewer displaying all baseline Primavera P6
    activities with real-time actual progress overlay.
  - What You See:
      * Activity ID, WBS Deliverable, Activity Name, Discipline, Area, Planned Start/Finish,
        and Duration.
      * Actual Execution Overlay: Actual Start/Finish dates derived from approved site updates.
      * Schedule Variance Badge: Colored indicators showing On-Track (0d), Ahead (-Nd),
        or Delayed (+Nd).
      * Linked Evidence Count: Number of field updates confirmed against each activity.
  - How to Use: Click any schedule activity row to open the Schedule Activity Drawer,
    displaying predecessor dependencies, linked supervisor updates, and variance analysis.

4.4 AI AUTO-MATCH MATRIX & PLANNER WORKBENCH
--------------------------------------------
  - Purpose: Lead Planner decision center for resolving ambiguous field updates,
    verifying matching evidence, and approving schedule links.
  - What You See:
      * Left Queue: List of pending updates ordered by uncertainty (lowest confidence first)
        with search and status filters.
      * Section 1: Field Event Details: Full verbatim field log, extracted summary,
        supervisor name, date, and attached photo indicator.
      * Section 2: AI Match & Milestone Assignment:
          - Top AI Recommendation: Suggested L5/L6 activity with confidence score badge
            and matching evidence breakdown chips (Keyword Match, Discipline Match,
            Spatial Area Match, Photo Evidence Tag).
          - Alternative Candidate Suggestions: 1-click alternative cards for rapid relinking.
          - Browse Full Schedule: Searchable activity picker for manual relinking across baseline.
      * Section 3: Prominent Action Bar:
          - Justification note input (recorded to immutable audit log).
          - Confirm Link (Green button) / Relink to Activity (Blue button).
          - Mark Unplanned (Amber button).
          - Reject Report (Red button).
  - How to Use: Select a candidate, review matching reasons, enter an audit note,
    and click the action button. The queue auto-advances smoothly.

4.5 FIELD PROGRESS & PHOTO LOGS (Supervisor Hub)
------------------------------------------------
  - Purpose: Dedicated site supervisor interface for capturing daily work logs,
    uploading construction photos, running OCR tag detection, and submitting progress.
  - What You See:
      * Discipline dropdown, Area / Location input, Event Status selector (Started,
        In Progress, Completed), Quantity field.
      * Extracted task description and verbatim supervisor log box.
      * Site blocker toggle with severity indicator.
      * Photo Evidence Upload Zone: Drag-and-drop box with file-picker fallback
        (JPEG, PNG, WebP <= 8 MB).
      * OCR Tag Scan Action: Runs local OCR on demand, returning candidate tags
        (e.g., "24-CW-017") with OCR recognition confidence.
      * Tag Confirmation Box: Candidate tag chips + manual tag input for confirming
        equipment tag before submission.
  - How to Use: Fill out report details, drop a photo, click "Run OCR Tag Scan",
    click the matching tag chip, and click "Ingest Field Record".

4.6 AI PREDICTIVE RISK & COPILOT (Schedule Risk Simulator)
----------------------------------------------------------
  - Purpose: Risk simulation and decision-support workspace featuring Datum's
    deterministic Scenario-Based Schedule Risk Simulator.
  - What You See:
      * Target Milestone Selector: Choose any baseline activity (e.g., "PIP-L6-012").
      * Simulated Delay Slider: Adjust simulated slippage from +1 to +60 working days.
      * Simulate Risk Scenario Button: Executes Finish-to-Start forward delay propagation.
      * Scenario Impact Summary: Downstream milestone cascade count, max completion slip,
        critical milestone impact flag, and risk level (Low, Med, High).
      * Downstream Domino Activity Chain Table: Exact activity-by-activity breakdown
        showing baseline window, new scenario window, incremental shift days, severity,
        and dependency relationship.
      * Executive Risk Briefing: Plain-language narrative ready for project controls reports.
  - How to Use: Select an activity, set the delay days, click "Simulate Scenario",
    and review the downstream cascade table.

4.7 DATA INGESTION & SCHEMAS

5. STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

WORKFLOW 1: SUBMITTING A FIELD REPORT WITH PHOTO & OCR TAG VERIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Navigate to "Field Progress & Photo Logs" in the sidebar.
  2. Select Discipline (e.g., "Piping"), enter Area (e.g., "Utility Yard Pump Bay"),
     and choose Status (e.g., "In Progress").
  3. Enter the description: "Erected line 24-CW-017 spool and verified alignment."
  4. In the Photo Evidence upload box, drag and drop the photo file (e.g., "FIELD_CW017_SPOOL.jpg").
  5. Notice the computed SHA-256 Fingerprint badge ("7f83b1657ff1fc...").
  6. Click "Run OCR Tag Scan".
  7. In the detected candidate chips, click "24-CW-017".
  8. Notice the confirmation banner: "Confirmed tag will be considered as matching
     evidence. It will not automatically approve the schedule link."
  9. Click "Ingest Field Record".
  10. A non-blocking success toast confirms ingestion.

WORKFLOW 2: REVIEWING AN AI MATCH AS LEAD PLANNER
-------------------------------------------------
  1. Switch to "Lead Planner Mode" using the top-right header button.
  2. Navigate to "AI Auto-Match Matrix".
  3. In the left queue, click update "UPD-001" or any pending report.
  4. Review the verbatim log text and verify the photo proof chip.
  5. In Section 2, inspect the Top AI Recommendation ("PIP-L6-012: Erect Line 24-CW-017")
     and matching breakdown chips (Keyword/Tag: 45/50, Discipline: 20/20, Spatial Area: 15/15).
  6. Enter an audit note in the justification box (e.g., "Verified pipe spool tag matches drawing CW-017").
  7. Click "Confirm Link" (Green button).
  8. The decision is saved, the audit log is updated, and the queue automatically advances.

WORKFLOW 3: RELINKING TO AN ALTERNATIVE MILESTONE
-------------------------------------------------
  1. If the top recommendation is not the intended deliverable, click one of the
     Alternative Candidate Suggestions (e.g., "PIP-L6-015") or use the Browse Full Schedule search.
  2. The blue radio button highlights the new selection.
  3. The action button updates to "Relink to PIP-L6-015" (Blue button).
  4. Enter an audit note explaining the re-assignment.
  5. Click "Relink".

WORKFLOW 4: MARKING OUT-OF-SCOPE WORK AS UNPLANNED
--------------------------------------------------
  1. If a field report describes temporary work or rework (e.g., "UPD-005: Fabricated temporary drain bypass"),
     select the update in the review queue.
  2. Enter an audit note (e.g., "Temporary drain line is out-of-scope rework; flagging for change order review").
  3. Click "Unplanned" (Amber button).
  4. The update is categorized as Unplanned Scope and surfaced on the Project Control Center blocker list.

WORKFLOW 5: SIMULATING A SCHEDULE DELAY SCENARIO
------------------------------------------------
  1. Navigate to "AI Predictive Risk & Copilot".
  2. Select target milestone "PIP-L6-012 (Erect Line 24-CW-017 Spool)".
  3. Move the Simulated Delay Duration slider to "+10 Days".
  4. Click "Simulate Risk Scenario".
  5. Inspect the Downstream Domino Activity Chain table to see that "PIP-L6-013 (Hydrotesting)"
     and "PIP-L6-014 (Pipe Insulation)" have shifted forward by 10 days.
  6. Review the generated executive briefing for distribution to project leadership.

6. UNDERSTANDING CONFIDENCE & MATCH CATEGORIES
================================================================================

Datum evaluates match confidence contextually for each individual field update
using a deterministic multi-factor algorithm. Confidence is never presented as
a single fixed project-wide number.

6.1 SCORE ATTRIBUTION BREAKDOWN (Max 100 Points)
------------------------------------------------
  - Keyword & Equipment Tag (Max 50 pts):
    Exact match of confirmed equipment tags ("24-CW-017"), isometric drawing numbers,
    or trade keywords ("pipe", "cable tray", "concrete").
  - Discipline Alignment (Max 20 pts):
    Exact match between report discipline ("Piping") and activity discipline ("Piping").
  - Spatial Area (Max 15 pts):
    Area/location keyword overlap ("Utility Yard", "Pump Bay", "Substation").
  - Fuzzy Token Similarity (Max 15 pts):
    Textual similarity between supervisor text and activity name/aliases.

6.2 MATCH DECISION THRESHOLDS
-----------------------------
  +------------------+------------------+--------+------------------------------------+
  | Score Range      | Category         | Color  | Action Required                    |
  +------------------+------------------+--------+------------------------------------+
  | >= 75%           | Ready to Link    | Green  | High confidence; 1-click approval. |
  | 40% - 74%        | Review Required  | Amber  | Ambiguous/close runner-up; review. |
  | < 40%            | Unplanned Scope  | Rose   | Low confidence; route to unplanned.|
  +------------------+------------------+--------+------------------------------------+

  CRITICAL RULE: AI and OCR recommendations NEVER auto-approve or auto-link activities.
  Final authority always rests with the human Lead Planner.

7. TROUBLESHOOTING & SYSTEM LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

  - Oversized Photo Error:
    Uploaded photo exceeds 8 MB. Compress image below 8 MB (JPEG/PNG/WebP).

  - OCR Detects No Tag:
    Photo is blurry or tag is obscured. Use manual tag input box to enter the tag
    (e.g., "24-CW-017") and click "Confirm Tag".

  - Backend Offline Indicator:
    Node.js backend server (localhost:5000) is not running. Datum automatically
    operates in client-side standalone fallback mode using local storage.
    To start backend, run `run_backend.bat`.

  - Restarting Fresh Demo:
    Testing completed and fresh baseline data is desired. Click "Restart Demo"
    in top-right header to restore benchmark schedule, sample reports, and photo proofs.
