1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In modern multi-billion-dollar infrastructure and EPC megaprojects (such as refineries,
metro rail networks, thermal power stations, and chemical plants), project execution
relies heavily on Master Schedule Baselines authored in Oracle Primavera P6 or
Microsoft Project (MSP). These baselines break down the project scope into thousands
of discrete Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) deliverables down to Level 5 (Package
Sub-elements) and Level 6 (Individual Field Activity Items).

THE CRITICAL INDUSTRY PAIN POINT:
While the Primavera P6 schedule specifies exact formal activity names, planned start/finish
dates, and engineering tags (e.g., "PIP-L6-012: Erect Line 24-CW-017"), actual on-site
execution is recorded in completely unstructured, fragmented formats:
  a) Daily Progress Reports (DPR) written in free-text by site supervisors (e.g.,
     "CW 24-inch spool was erected near Pump Bay today; alignment is in progress.")
  b) Discipline-specific Excel logs (e.g., piping contractor spreadsheets listing
     daily inch-dia welds, spool numbers, and joint statuses).
  c) Verbal updates, WhatsApp / radio transcripts, and shift handover diaries.

Because site supervisors use colloquial field jargon, informal acronyms, partial tag
numbers, and shorthand descriptions, project planners spend 15 to 25 hours every week
manually reading supervisor text, figuring out which Primavera P6 activity it belongs to,
and typing progress into the scheduling software.

CONSEQUENCES OF THE CURRENT MANUAL PROCESS:
1. Latency & Blind Spots: Planners are typically 3 to 7 days behind real site reality.
2. Inaccurate Delay Tracking: Schedule delay variances are recognized too late to take
   mitigating corrective actions (such as re-sequencing work or adding shifts).
3. Ghost Work & Unplanned Scope Creep: Field crews frequently perform out-of-scope work
   (e.g., temporary bypasses, minor drain lines, rework) that never gets attributed to
   the baseline, leading to contractor claims, disputes, and budget overruns.
4. Lack of Auditability: When disputes occur between project owners and contractors,
   there is no immutable audit trail connecting the supervisor's original written note
   to the approved schedule update.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROJECT AIM & CORE OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ProjectPulse solves this industry bottleneck by providing an end-to-end, AI-assisted,
explainable linking pipeline that ingests unstructured multi-format daily site updates,
intelligently matches them against L5/L6 Primavera P6 schedule deliverables with high
precision, calculates real-time schedule variances, and routes ambiguous items through an
ergonomic human-in-the-loop Planner Review workbench with full audit provenance.

PRIMARY TECHNICAL OBJECTIVES:
1. Multi-Source Ingestion:
   Seamlessly ingest raw free-text DPR files (.txt), structured contractor spreadsheets
   (.xlsx / .xls), and schedule baselines (.csv / Primavera export).
2. 4-Factor Deterministic & Explainable Matching Algorithm:
   Scores similarity from 0% to 100% using a transparent scoring rubric:
   - System Tag & Equipment Keyword Overlap (50% weight)
   - Engineering Discipline Consistency (20% weight)
   - Area / Location Proximity (15% weight)
   - Fuzzy Token Similarity (15% weight)
3. Intelligent Categorization & Confidence Routing:
   - Ready / Auto-Matched (Confidence >= 75%): High-confidence match with clear rationale.
   - Review Required (40% - 74% or multi-candidate ambiguity): Flagged for human planner review.
   - Unplanned Work (< 40% or explicit [Not in baseline] tag): Flagged as new out-of-scope work.
4. Schedule Performance & Variance Intelligence:
   Extract actual start and finish dates from site evidence, calculate progress percentages,
   and compute schedule delay variance in days (Actual Finish vs Planned Finish).
5. Human-in-the-Loop Planner Review Workbench:
   A dual-pane interactive interface enabling project planners to verify AI suggestions,
   search and relink candidate activities, classify work as unplanned, or reject false logs.
6. Immutable Audit Trail:
   Every auto-link, planner override, timestamp, and justification note is permanently
   logged in an audit ledger for contractual compliance and dispute resolution.
7. One-Click Compliance Export:
   Generate standard alignment CSV reports, Excel workbooks, and Microsoft Project XML
   exports that can be directly audited or fed back into Primavera P6 / MS Project.

  Dedicated full-page compliance ledger recording every auto-link, human override,

  judges through every major capability with presenter cheatsheet sidecards.

4. 11-STEP DETERMINISTIC GUIDED DEMO EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Guided Demo (`GuidedDemoWalkthroughView.tsx`) provides a 100% deterministic, zero-risk,
high-impact presentation mode designed specifically for hackathon judging:

  Step 1: The Baseline Schedule (The Source of Truth)
    - Concept: Establishes the Primavera P6 master schedule structure (WBS, activities, planned dates).
    - Talk Track: "Megaprojects have thousands of structured activities, but site progress arrives in messy text."

  Step 2: Unstructured Site Progress Arrives (The Problem)
    - Concept: Ingests raw supervisor text ("CW 24-inch spool erected near Pump Bay...") full of field jargon.
    - Talk Track: "Notice how informal field terminology diverges completely from formal P6 activity names."

  Step 3: AI Activity Matching & Confidence Scoring (The Solution)
    - Concept: Real-time 6-point extraction analysis (Entity Extraction, Tag Resolution, Discipline Check,
      Area Proximity, S-Curve Progress, Variance Detection).
    - Talk Track: "The engine breaks down the text in milliseconds without hallucination."

  Step 4: Explainable Matching Criteria (Explainability)
    - Concept: Transparent breakdown of the 4 scoring factors (50% Keywords, 20% Discipline, 15% Area, 15% Fuzzy).
    - Talk Track: "Unlike black-box LLMs, ProjectPulse scores every dimension with full mathematical transparency."

  Step 5: High-Confidence Auto-Linking (Automation)
    - Concept: Evaluates 91% confidence score and auto-links update to `PIP-L6-012` (Erect 24-CW-017).
    - Talk Track: "Clear updates with >= 75% confidence are matched instantly, saving 80% of planner time."

  Step 6: Visualizing the Connected Bridge (The Connection)
    - Concept: Animated visual bridge connecting the supervisor's field text directly to the P6 WBS node.
    - Talk Track: "Contractors, planners, and clients now share a single connected truth."

  Step 7: Schedule Intelligence & Delay Tracking (Impact)
    - Concept: Detects that planned progress was 80% while actual is 60%, flagging a 5-day variance delay.
    - Talk Track: "Instant variance detection prevents surprises before they become critical-path disasters."

  Step 8: Handling Uncertainty & Ambiguity (The Edge Case)
    - Concept: Tests an ambiguous update ("Erected cooling line near turbine...") yielding 52%, 48%, 44% scores.
    - Talk Track: "When confidence is below 75% or ambiguous, the AI halts auto-linking and requests human review."

  Step 9: Human-in-the-Loop Planner Review Workbench (Human Control)
    - Concept: Planner inspects top 3 candidates and clicks 'Link to Selected Activity' or writes a note.
    - Talk Track: "Human planners retain ultimate authority with one-click verification."

  Step 10: Handling Unplanned Work (Scope Creep Detection)
    - Concept: Identifies out-of-scope drain line installation (`[Not in baseline]`), flagging contractor scope creep.
    - Talk Track: "ProjectPulse automatically isolates ghost work to prevent untracked budget overruns."

  Step 11: Audit Trail & Project Governance (Accountability)
    - Concept: Displays the chronological ledger recording every AI match, planner approval, and override.
    - Talk Track: "Complete auditability for ISO compliance, claims defense, and dispute resolution."

  Final Step: Demo Summary & ROI Dashboard
    - 5 Core Takeaways (Zero Hallucination, 80% Time Saved, Scope Creep Protection, Full Auditability, ERP/P6 Ready).
    - Interactive "Restart Demo" or "Explore Live Platform" options.

5. USER ACCOUNTS & ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ProjectPulse provides two specialized personas tailored to industrial workflows:

  1. Lead Project Planner (P6 Administrator)
     - Username: planner@projectpulse.ai
     - Access: Full access to Dashboard, Review Queue, Schedule Activities,
       Site Updates, Audit Trail, and MSP/CSV export functions.
     - Role: Verifies AI suggestions, overrides links, approves variances.

  2. Site Field Supervisor (Execution Crew)
     - Username: supervisor@projectpulse.ai
     - Access: Simplified field report entry form, photo/evidence upload,
       daily progress logging, and view of submitted field reports.
     - Role: Submits raw daily progress logs, quantities installed, and site notes.
