1. THE BIG PICTURE: HOW THIS ENTIRE SYSTEM WORKS (IN PLAIN ENGLISH)
================================================================================
Imagine a construction project building a massive cooling water pump house:
- The Head Office has a MASTER SCHEDULE (in Primavera P6 or Excel). It has strict,
  formal activity codes, like:
  "PIP-L6-012: Erect Line 24-CW-017 (Planned: Sept 5 to Sept 7, Area: Pump Bay)".
- But on the physical construction site, the site supervisor writes down in their
  daily log:
  "3. CW 24-inch spool was erected near Pump Bay today; alignment is in progress."
  Or the contractor sends an Excel sheet with row: "Spool 24-CW-017, Status: Started".

Notice the problem?
The site supervisor did NOT write "PIP-L6-012". They used words like "CW 24-inch spool",
"alignment in progress", or "spool erected".
Before this software was built, a human planner had to spend hours reading hundreds of
such messy notes and manually finding which schedule line item it matched.

HOW PROJECTPULSE AUTOMATES THIS:
1. Multi-Source Ingestion: The system reads the schedule CSV, the supervisor's text notes,
   and the Excel tracking sheets.
2. Intelligent NLP Matching Engine: For every supervisor update, it runs a 4-factor test:
   - Does it mention equipment tags or aliases? ("24-CW-017", "CW spool" -> up to 50 pts)
   - Does the discipline match? (Piping vs Civil vs Electrical -> up to 20 pts)
   - Is it in the same physical area? ("Pump Bay" vs "Filter Bay" -> up to 15 pts)
   - Do the action words match? ("erected", "pour", "excavation" -> up to 15 pts)
   Total score = 0 to 100%.
3. Three-Way Routing:
   - High Confidence (>= 75%): Automatically approved and linked.
   - Ambiguous / Medium (40% to 74%): Sent to the PLANNER REVIEW QUEUE so a human can
     make the final call in 1 click.
   - Low Confidence (< 40%) or "[Not in baseline]": Flagged as UNPLANNED WORK (work done
     on site that was never scheduled).
4. Real-Time Schedule Update: Once linked, the software calculates:
   - Did the activity start on time?
   - Is it completed or in progress?
   - How many days is it delayed compared to the planned finish date?
5. Audit Trail: Every single match, approval, or change is permanently recorded so nobody
   can dispute who approved what.

2. WHAT ARE YOU LOOKING AT? SCREEN-BY-SCREEN WALKTHROUGH

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 THE TOP NAVIGATION BAR (NAVBAR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located at the very top of your screen:
- "ProjectPulse [SIH 2026]": The platform brand.
- "Backend: SQLite Connected" (Pill): Shows the live status of the backend REST API
  and local SQLite database. If the backend is running, it pulses green. If offline, it
  gracefully switches to "Client Mode (Standalone)".
- 5 Navigation Tabs:
  1. Dashboard: Overall executive health and delay summary.
  2. Site Updates: The table of all parsed daily supervisor notes and Excel rows.
  3. Schedule Activities: The master baseline schedule (L5/L6) with planned vs actuals.
  4. Planner Review: The queue of uncertain items waiting for your verification.
     (Shows an amber badge count with the number of pending items!)
  5. Upload / Demo Data: Where you can upload custom files or reload the demo data.
- "Export CSV" Button: Downloads the complete schedule alignment report as a verified CSV.
- "Reload Demo" Button: Resets all data back to the clean benchmark project state.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 DASHBOARD (EXECUTIVE KPI & SCHEDULE HEALTH OVERVIEW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you first open the app, you are greeted by the Dashboard. Here is what you see:

A. ACTION REQUIRED CALLOUT BANNER (Top):
   - If there are items in the Planner Review Queue, a large amber warning banner alerts
     you: "Action Required: X Site Updates Pending Review". Clicking "Open Review Queue"
     takes you directly to the review workbench.
   - If all items have been reviewed and approved, it turns green: "All Site Updates
     Verified & Aligned".

B. 5 CORE KPI METRIC CARDS:
   1. Extracted Site Updates: Total number of discrete daily work items parsed from
      both supervisor text notes and Excel sheets (e.g. 14 items).
   2. Verified & Linked: Number of site items successfully linked to an approved schedule
      activity ID (e.g. 10 items).
   3. Pending Review: Number of site items that are ambiguous or have moderate confidence
      scores and need human confirmation (e.g. 2 items).
   4. Unplanned Activities: Number of site items that represent out-of-scope work (e.g.
      a small-bore drain line not included in the original project schedule).
   5. Schedule Delays / Variances: Number of schedule activities whose site progress
      indicates they are behind the baseline planned finish date.

C. DISCIPLINE COMPLETION & SCHEDULE HEALTH CARDS:
   - Shows horizontal progress bars for each engineering package: Civil, Piping,
     Electrical, Instrumentation, and HSE.
   - Displays percentage completed, number of completed activities, and delayed count.

D. SCHEDULE VARIANCE WATCHLIST (Bottom-Left Table):
   - A prioritized table showing only activities that are behind schedule!
   - Columns: Activity ID, Activity Name, Discipline, Planned Finish, Variance (+days).
   - Clicking ANY row opens the Schedule Activity Drawer to investigate the cause!

E. LIVE AUDIT TRAIL FEED (Bottom-Right Card):
   - A streaming feed of recent ingestion actions, auto-links, and planner approvals.
   - Clicking any audit row opens the Site Update Inspector to view the original text.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 SITE UPDATES VIEW (EXTRACTED FIELD EVIDENCE TABLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clicking the "Site Updates" tab opens the master table of all raw site evidence.
- Top Toolbar:
  * Live Search Box: Type any keyword, equipment tag, supervisor name, or area.
  * Discipline Dropdown: Filter by Civil, Piping, Electrical, Instrumentation, HSE.
  * Status Dropdown: Filter by Linked/Approved, Review Needed, Unplanned Work.
  * Source Dropdown: Filter by daily_report.txt or piping_progress.xlsx.
  * Sort Dropdown: Sort by Date (newest/oldest) or Confidence (highest/lowest).

- Table Columns:
  1. Update ID: Unique system identifier (e.g. "TXT-LINE-3" or "XLSX-ROW-1").
  2. Date: The date the work was performed on site (e.g. "2026-09-05").
  3. Source & Line: Which file it came from and the line or row number (#3).
  4. Discipline: The engineering package (Civil, Piping, Electrical, HSE).
  5. Extracted Work Description: Cleaned summary of the work done.
  6. Area: Physical location on site (e.g. "Pump Bay", "Fabrication Yard").
  7. Event Status: "Started", "In Progress", or "Completed".
  8. Matched L5/L6 Activity: The green Activity ID it was linked to, or an amber
     "Needs Review" badge, or a red "Unplanned" badge.
  9. Confidence: A progress bar and score (0–100%) showing algorithm certainty.
  10. Action Button:
      - If it needs review: Yellow "Review >" button (opens Planner Review).
      - If already linked: Gray "Inspect" button (opens the Inspector Drawer).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 SCHEDULE ACTIVITIES VIEW (MASTER BASELINE & VARIANCE TRACKER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clicking "Schedule Activities" shows the master project plan (what SHOULD happen):
- Table Columns:
  1. Activity ID: Formal schedule code (e.g. "PIP-L6-012").
  2. WBS: Work Breakdown Structure hierarchy number (e.g. "2.1.2").
  3. Activity Name & Aliases: Formal name ("Erect Line 24-CW-017") plus recognized
     synonyms and equipment tags below it ("spool erection", "CW line").
  4. Discipline: Engineering branch (Piping, Civil, etc.).
  5. Area: Designated physical work zone ("Pump Bay").
  6. Planned Window: Start date to finish date ("2026-09-05 -> 2026-09-07").
  7. Actual Evidence: Extracted site dates ("2026-09-05 -> Active" or "Completed").
  8. Variance: Days of delay!
     * "+2d" (Red Badge): Activity finished or is running 2 days late.
     * "0d" (Slate Badge): On track according to schedule.
     * "-1d" (Green Badge): Finished 1 day ahead of schedule.
  9. Status: "Not Started", "In Progress", "Completed", or "Delayed".
  10. Linked Evidence: Number of site supervisor notes or Excel rows linked to this.
  11. Action: "Details >" button to open the deep-dive drawer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 PLANNER REVIEW WORKBENCH (AI REVIEW & SCHEDULE LINKER QUEUE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the core cockpit for the Project Planner. It has a dual-pane layout:

A. LEFT PANE — THE REVIEW QUEUE:
   - Shows all items waiting for planner attention.
   - Filter pills: "Review" (ambiguous items), "Unplanned" (out-of-scope work),
     "Approved" (resolved items), "All".
   - Search bar: Quick search within the queue.
   - Each queue card shows: Update ID, Confidence Score %, Description, Discipline,
     and Source File.
   - Sorted by lowest confidence first, so the hardest questions come first!

B. RIGHT PANE — THE VERIFICATION WORKBENCH:
   When you click an item on the left, the right pane displays:
   1. Header: Update ID, Discipline, Area, Date, Source File & Line #.
   2. Raw Site Evidence Card: Exactly what the supervisor wrote in quotes.
   3. NLP Match Score Breakdown: Shows how the AI calculated the score:
      - Keyword Match (out of 50 pts)
      - Discipline Match (out of 20 pts)
      - Area Proximity (out of 15 pts)
      - Fuzzy Similarity (out of 15 pts)
      - Bullet points listing exact reasons (e.g. "System tag match: Cooling Water (CW)").
   4. Interactive Schedule Linker:
      - A search bar allowing you to search ANY schedule activity by ID or keyword.
      - A list of candidate activities with radio buttons. The AI's top suggestion has
        a green "★ AI Recommended" badge.
   5. Planner Audit Note: A text box where you can type an optional justification note
      (e.g. "Confirmed with supervisor that spool 24-CW-017 was erected today").
   6. Action Buttons:
      - "Confirm Link to [Activity ID]" (Blue Button): Approves the link! The item is
        instantly linked, the schedule updates, an audit record is saved, and the queue
        automatically advances to the next item!
      - "Classify as Unplanned Work" (Amber Button): Confirms this was new out-of-scope
        work that is NOT part of the baseline schedule.
      - "Reject Report" (Gray Button): Discards invalid, mistaken, or duplicate reports.

3. EXHAUSTIVE FIELD-BY-FIELD GLOSSARY (WHAT EVERY TERM MEANS)
================================================================================

1. Activity ID:
   The unique identifier for a master schedule line item in Primavera P6.
   Format: [DISCIPLINE]-[LEVEL]-[SEQUENCE], e.g. "PIP-L6-012" = Piping, Level 6, #12.

2. WBS (Work Breakdown Structure):
   The hierarchical code defining the project tree.
   Example: "2.1.2" represents:
     2 = Piping Package (L3)
     2.1 = Cooling Water Underground Piping (L4)
     2.1.2 = Above-ground Spool Erection (L5/L6)

3. Activity Name:
   The formal contractual description of the work package.
   Example: "Erect Line 24-CW-017".

4. Aliases:
   Alternative terms, nicknames, informal abbreviations, or equipment tags used by
   crews in the field to refer to an activity.
   Example for PIP-L6-012: "spool erection; erect cooling water line; CW line erection".

5. Discipline:
   The engineering department or trade responsible for the work.
   Values: Civil, Piping, Electrical, Instrumentation, HSE (Health, Safety & Environment).

6. Planned Start / Planned Finish:
   The baseline calendar dates established in the contractual schedule when work was
   supposed to start and complete.

7. Actual Start / Actual Finish:
   The real calendar dates extracted from verified on-site supervisor daily reports.
   - If work was reported "Started", the report date becomes Actual Start.
   - If work was reported "Completed", the report date becomes Actual Finish.

8. Variance (Days Delay):
   The mathematical difference between actual performance and planned baseline:
   Formula: Variance = (Actual Finish Date - Planned Finish Date) in days.
   - Positive (+2d): 2 days behind schedule (DELAYED).
   - Zero (0d): Exactly on schedule (ON TRACK).
   - Negative (-1d): Finished 1 day earlier than planned (AHEAD OF SCHEDULE).

9. Confidence Score (0% to 100%):
   A deterministic similarity score produced by the AI matching engine based on:
   - Keyword Score (0 to 50): Tag, line number, and alias match.
   - Discipline Score (0 to 20): Discipline agreement.
   - Area Score (0 to 15): Site location match.
   - Fuzzy Score (0 to 15): General vocabulary token overlap.

10. Match Category:
    - Ready: High confidence (>= 75%). Can be auto-approved.
    - Review: Medium confidence (40% to 74%) or ambiguous text (e.g. "cable pulling"
      without feeder tag). Requires human planner verification.
    - Unplanned: Low confidence (< 40%) or explicit out-of-baseline marker.
    - Rejected: Invalid or mistaken report discarded by the planner.

11. Event Status:
    The completion status reported from the field:
    - "Started": Work has commenced on site.
    - "In Progress": Work is ongoing (alignment, welding, etc.).
    - "Completed": Work is 100% installed, poured, or inspected.

12. Unplanned Activity:
    Work executed by field teams that has NO corresponding line item in the master
    contract schedule. Crucial for identifying contractor scope creep or claims early!

13. Line Evidence:
    The exact physical line number in daily_report.txt or row number in the Excel sheet
    where the raw data originated, preserving full audit integrity.

14. Audit Log:
    A permanent, append-only record capturing every decision, timestamp, user, original
    score, and rationale note for legal, contractual, and audit compliance.

4. STEP-BY-STEP TUTORIALS: HOW TO USE THE SYSTEM
================================================================================

TUTORIAL 1: HOW TO REVIEW & APPROVE PENDING SITE UPDATES
1. Look at the top Navbar or Dashboard banner. Notice the amber badge: "X Items Need Review".
2. Click the "Planner Review" tab.
3. On the left queue, click the first pending item (e.g. "TXT-LINE-9: Crew reported cable pulling...").
4. Read the raw quote in the "Raw Site Evidence" card. Notice that the crew said "cable
   pulling", but didn't mention the feeder number.
5. In the "Link to L5/L6 Baseline Schedule Activity" section below, look at the AI recommendations.
   You see:
   - ELE-L6-022: Pull MCC feeder cable (Area: Pump Bay)
6. Click the radio button next to ELE-L6-022.
7. In the "Planner Audit Note" box, type:
   "Confirmed with electrical foreman that this was the main MCC feeder in Pump Bay."
8. Click the blue button: "Confirm Link to ELE-L6-022".
9. That's it! The update is approved, the schedule is updated, an audit entry is saved,
   and the queue automatically advances to the next pending item!

TUTORIAL 2: HOW TO CLASSIFY OUT-OF-SCOPE / UNPLANNED WORK
1. In the "Planner Review" tab, select an item like "TXT-LINE-6: A small-bore drain line was installed...".
2. Notice the confidence score is low (15%) because "drain line" is not in the schedule baseline.
3. In the "Planner Audit Note" box, type:
   "Field modification requested by client; not in baseline scope. Adding to variation register."
4. Click the amber button: "Classify as Unplanned Work".
5. The item is safely marked as Unplanned, and the Dashboard KPI for "Unplanned Activities"
   increments to alert management of scope creep!

TUTORIAL 3: HOW TO INVESTIGATE A DELAYED ACTIVITY
1. Open the "Dashboard" tab.
2. Scroll down to the "Schedule Variance Watchlist" table on the left.
3. Find an activity with a red badge like "+2d" (e.g. CIV-L6-001: Excavate pump foundation).
4. Click on the row. The "Schedule Activity Drawer" slides open from the right.
5. In the drawer, compare:
   - Planned Window: 2026-09-01 -> 2026-09-03 (3 days)
   - Actual Site Window: 2026-09-05 (Completed 2 days late!)
6. Look at the bottom section: "Linked Site Progress Updates (1)".
7. Click "Inspect" next to the linked note to see the supervisor's original daily report!

TUTORIAL 4: HOW TO EXPORT THE OFFICIAL COMPLIANCE REPORT
1. Go to the top navigation bar.
2. Click the "Export CSV" button.
3. The browser will instantly download a clean file named:
   `SIH_122_Schedule_Alignment_2026-09-05.csv`.
4. Open it in Microsoft Excel. You will see every update ID, date, discipline, full description,
   matched activity ID, confidence score, and planner audit notes, ready for executive reporting!
