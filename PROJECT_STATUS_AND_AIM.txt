1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In modern multi-billion-dollar infrastructure and EPC megaprojects (such as refineries,
metro rail systems, fertilizer plants, thermal power stations, and chemical plants),
project management relies heavily on Master Schedule Baselines (typically authored in
Oracle Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project). These baselines break down the scope of work
into thousands of discrete Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) deliverables down to Level 5
(L5 - Package Sub-elements) and Level 6 (L6 - Individual Field Activity Items).

THE CRITICAL INDUSTRY PAIN POINT:
While the Primavera P6 schedule specifies exact formal activity names, planned start
dates, planned finish dates, and engineering tags (e.g. "PIP-L6-012: Erect Line 24-CW-017"),
actual on-site execution is captured in completely unstructured, fragmented formats:
  a) Daily Progress Reports (DPR) written in free-text by site supervisors (e.g.
     "CW 24-inch spool was erected near Pump Bay today; alignment is in progress.")
  b) Discipline-specific Excel logs (e.g. piping contractor spreadsheets listing
     daily inch-dia welds, spool numbers, and joint statuses).
  c) Verbal updates, WhatsApp / radio transcripts, and shift handover diaries.

Because site supervisors use colloquial field jargon, informal acronyms, partial tag
numbers, and shorthand descriptions, project planners spend 15 to 25 hours every week
manually reading supervisor text, figuring out which Primavera P6 activity it belongs to,
and typing progress into the scheduling software. 

CONSEQUENCES OF THE CURRENT MANUAL PROCESS:
1. Latency & Blind Spots: Planners are typically 3 to 7 days behind real site reality.
2. Inaccurate Delay Tracking: Schedule delay variances are recognized too late to take
   mitigating corrective actions (such as re-sequencing work or adding shifts).
3. Ghost Work & Unplanned Scope Creep: Field crews frequently perform out-of-scope work
   (e.g. temporary bypasses, minor drain lines, rework) that never gets attributed to
   the baseline, leading to contractor claims, disputes, and budget overruns.
4. Lack of Auditability: When disputes occur between project owners and contractors,
   there is no immutable audit trail connecting the supervisor's original written note
   to the approved schedule update.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROJECT AIM & CORE OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ProjectPulse solves this industry bottleneck by providing an end-to-end, AI-powered,
deterministic linking pipeline that ingests unstructured multi-format daily site updates,
intelligently matches them against L5/L6 Primavera P6 schedule deliverables with high
precision, calculates real-time schedule variances, and routes ambiguous items through an
ergonomic human-in-the-loop Planner Review workbench with full audit provenance.

PRIMARY TECHNICAL OBJECTIVES:
1. Multi-Source Ingestion:
   Seamlessly ingest raw free-text DPR files (.txt), structured contractor spreadsheets
   (.xlsx / .xls), and schedule baselines (.csv / Primavera export).
2. 4-Factor Deterministic Matching Algorithm:
   Score similarity from 0% to 100% using a transparent, explainable scoring rubric:
   - System Tag & Equipment Keyword Overlap (max 50 points)
   - Engineering Discipline Consistency (max 20 points)
   - Area / Location Proximity (max 15 points)
   - Fuzzy Token Similarity (max 15 points)
3. Intelligent Categorization:
   - Ready (Confidence >= 75%): Automatically approved and linked.
   - Review (40% - 74% or ambiguous text): Flagged for human planner review.
   - Unplanned (< 40% or explicit [Not in baseline] tag): Flagged as new out-of-scope work.
4. Schedule Performance & Variance Enrichment:
   Extract actual start and finish dates from site evidence, calculate progress percentages,
   and compute schedule delay variance in days (Actual Finish vs Planned Finish).
5. Human-in-the-Loop Planner Review Workbench:
   A dual-pane interactive interface enabling project planners to verify AI suggestions,
   search and relink candidate activities, classify work as unplanned, or reject false logs.
6. Immutable Audit Trail:
   Every auto-link, planner override, timestamp, and justification note is permanently
   logged in an audit ledger for contractual compliance and dispute resolution.
7. One-Click Compliance Export:
   Generate standard alignment CSV reports that can be directly audited or fed back
   into Primavera P6 / MS Project.

4. CURRENT STATUS & COMPLETED FEATURES (WHAT WE HAVE BUILT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[100% COMPLETE] 1. Data Ingestion & Parser Engine:
   - CSV Schedule Parser: Extracts Activity ID, WBS, Activity Name, Discipline,
     Planned Start, Planned Finish, Area, and semicolon-delimited Aliases.
   - Free-Text DPR Parser: Parses daily_report.txt, extracts report date, discipline
     section headers (Civil, Piping, Electrical, HSE), numbered site progress items,
     detects "[Not in baseline]" markers, infers event status, area, and line evidence.
   - Excel Discipline Progress Parser: Reads piping_progress.xlsx, converts Excel serial
     dates to ISO dates, extracts Entry ID, status, quantities, units, and supervisor names.

[100% COMPLETE] 2. 4-Factor Deterministic Matching Algorithm:
   - Evaluates keyword overlap (system tags like "24-CW-017", "CW", "FW", "Suction"),
     discipline match, area proximity, and token similarity.
   - Ambiguity detection: automatically flags vague reports like "cable pulling" or
     close candidate scores for human Planner Review rather than guessing dangerously.

[100% COMPLETE] 3. Schedule Performance & Variance Enrichment:
   - Computes actual start date, actual finish date, progress percentage, and variance
     in days (delay vs planned finish).
   - Generates status: "Not Started", "In Progress", "Completed", "Delayed".

[100% COMPLETE] 4. Executive Dashboard:
   - 5 KPI Metric Cards: Extracted Site Updates, Verified & Linked, Pending Review,
     Unplanned Activities, Schedule Delays.
   - Discipline Completion & Health Progress Bars (Civil, Piping, Electrical, HSE).
   - Schedule Variance Watchlist displaying critical delayed activities.
   - Live Audit Trail feed of recent actions.

[100% COMPLETE] 5. Site Updates Table View:
   - Full data table of all ingested updates with live keyword search.
   - Filters by Discipline, Status (Approved, Review, Unplanned), and Source File.
   - Sorting by Date and Confidence Score.
   - Direct inspection and review action triggers.

[100% COMPLETE] 6. Master Schedule Activities Table View:
   - Baseline view with WBS codes, planned dates, actual dates, variance badges,
     and recognized aliases.
   - Multi-field search and discipline/area filters.

[100% COMPLETE] 7. Interactive Planner Review Workbench:
   - Dual-pane layout: Left queue sorted by lowest confidence / priority; Right workbench
     with source text evidence, NLP score breakdown, and interactive schedule activity linker.
   - Action buttons: "Confirm Link", "Classify as Unplanned Work", "Reject Report".
   - Optional planner audit note recording.
   - Auto-advance to next queue item upon action completion.

[100% COMPLETE] 8. Slide-Over Drawers & Audit Modal:
   - Inspector Drawer: Inspect raw evidence, review score breakdown, edit extracted

5. OFFICIAL BENCHMARK DATASET COVERAGE (DEMO DATA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project is validated against the official SIH 122 Cooling Water Pump Package dataset:
  1. schedule.csv: 16 L5/L6 baseline activities spanning Civil (1.1.1–1.1.4), Piping
     (2.1.1–2.1.6), Electrical (3.1.1–3.1.4), Instrumentation (4.1.1), and HSE (5.1.1).
  2. daily_report.txt: Free-text supervisor notes covering pump foundation excavation,
     PCC concreting, CW 24-inch spool erection, spool fabrication in yard, FW spool erection,
     out-of-scope drain line installation [Not in baseline], cable tray installation,
     earthing strip work, ambiguous cable pulling report, and crane lifting inspection.
  3. piping_progress.xlsx: Detailed Excel tracking sheet with quantities, inch-dia welds,
     supervisors, and status codes.
